CC0 recipe - basic pancakes (placeholder).

Combine one cup of flour, one tablespoon of sugar, two teaspoons of
baking powder, one egg, and one cup of milk. Whisk until smooth. Cook
spoonfuls on a hot greased pan until the edges are dry, then flip and
cook the other side for about a minute. Serves two.
